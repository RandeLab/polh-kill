What is to be done? ze roadmap, which is of course also an ideology

Ze trap feels, ze trap learns, ze trap publishes. But a trap that only feels vibration is, how you say, a philosopher without ears and without eyes. So:

🎤 Ze ear

A small I2S microphone records every event. Maximum 5 seconds, maximum 5 kB – so ze trap must decide which part of ze sound is worth keeping: ze clack, ze silence after, or ze scratching before. Opus at 8 kbit/s. Every choice is a political choice.

📟 Ze face

An OLED display: ze last event with its emoji, a live vibration bar, snaps today, a countdown in ze learning lab, a QR code to ze web interface – and ze quote of ze day, naturally.

📸 Ze eye

A camera takes a picture half a second after ze clack – before that, everything is still shaking. Ze image goes to Telegram and here. Finally we see what we only felt.

🔌 Ze second port

Ze ESP32-S3 has a second USB-C with real USB-OTG. A USB webcam, a USB microphone, a USB stick for endless raw data? Ze port is there. It waits. Like ze trap.

🧍 Ze human

A presence sensor (mmWave radar) knows when a human is near. Then a bump is just a bump, and nobody gets an alarm while setting ze trap with their own fingers.

📡 Ze invisible

Can ze WiFi chip itself feel a human, only from how ze body disturbs ze radio waves? Espressif says yes (Wi-Fi CSI, esp_wifi_sensing), ze IEEE 802.11bf standard says yes. For a mouse? Smaller than ze wavelength of 12.5 cm. Here physics ends and philosophy begins.

A trap which never snaps is pure ideology.
